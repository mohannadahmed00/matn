About this Matn

A foundational didactic poem (matn) for students beginning their journey in the science of Tajweed. Written by Imam Sulayman al-Jamzuri, it concisely explains the rules of Noon Sakinah, Tanween, Meem Sakinah, and Madd in rhythmic Arabic verse, making it ideal for memorization and preservation of the sacred tradition.
